**Status:** Strategic Implementation Guide

## Core Insight

Talent Graph runs **two businesses in parallel**:

1. **A services business** (search firm) — generates revenue from Day 1
2. **A data business** (Talent Graph + Trust Graph) — builds the moat over time

The platform is a **byproduct of doing the services business well**. Every placement feeds data into the graph. The product captures intelligence as a side effect of doing placements, not as a separate activity.

---

## The 6 Business Logic Engines

---

### Engine 1: Role Decomposition Engine

When a client says "I need a Senior PM," most recruiters post a JD. Talent Graph **decomposes the role into a structured Hiring DNA**.

**Logic:**

```
Client says: "Senior PM for payments"

System captures:
├── Must-Have Skills: [payments domain, B2B SaaS, 0→1 experience]
├── Nice-to-Have: [fintech regulatory, international expansion]
├── Experience Signal: [5-8 years, at least 1 scale-up]
├── Impact Threshold: [managed P&L > 10Cr OR user base > 1M]
├── Leadership Signal: [managed team of 3+, hired at least 2 PMs]
├── Culture Fit: [ownership-driven, low-process tolerance]
└── Anti-Patterns: [job-hopper (<18mo avg), only large-corp experience]
```

**Why this matters:**
- The Hiring DNA becomes the **search query** against the Talent Graph
- Every role decomposed makes future matching faster — you build a library of role archetypes
- After 20-30 roles, you can offer "Role Templates" to clients

**Implementation timeline:**
- Phase 1: Structured form (dropdowns for skills, sliders for thresholds)
- Phase 2: AI-assisted suggestions based on similar past roles
- Phase 3: Auto-generate Hiring DNA from a job description

### Engine 2: Candidate Intelligence Engine (The Talent Graph)

This is NOT a database. It's a **knowledge graph**. The difference matters.

| Database stores | Knowledge graph stores |
|---|---|
| Name, company, title, skills | This person scaled a payments product from 0 to 50Cr ARR, managed 12 engineers, was promoted twice in 3 years, and 3 people they managed went on to become Directors |

**Data capture logic (on every candidate interaction):**

```
├── What they TELL you (self-reported)
├── What you VERIFY (reference checks, public data)
├── What you INFER (career velocity, impact trajectory)
└── What the NETWORK says (trust edges, referral quality)
```

**Career Velocity Calculation (key differentiator):**

```
velocity_score = (
  promotion_count / years_of_experience         * 0.3
  + scope_increase_rate                          * 0.3
  + brand_progression_quality                    * 0.2
  + lateral_move_penalty                         * -0.1
  + tenure_consistency                           * 0.1
)
```

| Component | What it measures |
|---|---|
| `promotion_count / years` | How fast they move up |
| `scope_increase_rate` | Team size growth, budget growth over time |
| `brand_progression_quality` | Startup → scale-up → enterprise trajectory |
| `lateral_move_penalty` | Too many lateral moves = red flag |
| `tenure_consistency` | Average tenure > 2 years = stable |

**Implementation timeline:**
- Phase 1: Structured form — recruiter fills in after every candidate call (accept the data entry cost)
- Phase 2: Parse LinkedIn profiles and auto-populate 60% of fields. Recruiter validates and enriches.
- Phase 3: AI extracts impact signals from conversation transcripts

### Engine 3: Matching & Scoring Engine

Where Hiring DNA meets Talent Graph.

**Step 1: Hard Filter (binary pass/fail)**

```
- Must-have skills present?
- Experience years in range?
- Compensation expectation within budget?
- Location/remote compatible?
- Status != not_looking?

→ Candidates who fail ANY = excluded
```

**Step 2: Soft Score (0-100 per dimension)**

| Dimension | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Role Fit | 40% | Skill overlap, experience depth, domain match |
| Evidence | 25% | Verified impact metrics vs role's impact threshold |
| Leadership | 15% | Team size, hiring experience, budget ownership |
| Trust | 10% | Referral quality, network endorsements, "would hire again" count |
| Evaluation | 10% | Recruiter's subjective assessment after conversation |

**Composite Score Formula:**

```
composite = (roleFit * 0.4) + (evidence * 0.25) + (leadership * 0.15)
          + (trust * 0.1) + (evaluation * 0.1)
```

**Step 3:** Rank by composite score, surface top 15-20 for recruiter review.

**Critical Rule:** Scores are **recruiter-assisted, not auto-generated**. The system suggests scores based on data overlap. The recruiter confirms. This keeps quality high.

**The "Would You Hire Again?" Signal:** When someone says "I would hire this person again," that's worth more than any skill assessment. Track this aggressively.

### Engine 4: Trust Graph Engine

This is the moat. Here's the actual logic.

**Edge Types and Weights:**

| Edge Type | Direction | Weight | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `referred` | Directed (A→B) | 3 | "I vouch for this person" |
| `worked_with` | Bidirectional | 2 | "I know their work" |
| `managed` | Directed (A→B) | 4 | "I was responsible for their growth" |
| `would_hire_again` | Directed (A→B) | 5 | "I'd bet on them again" — **STRONGEST SIGNAL** |

**Trust Propagation (Phase 2+):**

```
If A referred B (trust = 3)
And B referred C (trust = 3)
Then A→C has indirect trust = 3 * 3 * decay_factor(0.3) = 2.7

Rules:
- Trust decays with distance
- Maximum 2 hops
- This is how you discover candidates through the network
```

**Referrer Reliability Score:**

```
reliability = (
  candidates_hired / candidates_referred       * 0.5
  + candidates_shortlisted / candidates_referred * 0.3
  + avg_composite_score_of_referrals / 100       * 0.2
)
```

**Example comparison:**

| Referrer | Referred | Shortlisted | Hired | Reliability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Referrer A | 10 | 3 | 1 | Lower (volume, low conversion) |
| Referrer B | 3 | 2 | 1 | Higher (quality over quantity) |

Quality > volume. This is intentional.

**Implementation timeline:**
- Phase 1: Manual edge creation — recruiter logs relationships during candidate conversations
- Phase 2: Auto-suggest edges — "You and X both worked at Razorpay 2021-2023. Did you work together?"
- Phase 3: Trust propagation algorithm runs nightly, surfaces hidden candidates

### Engine 5: Pipeline & Placement Engine

This is the revenue engine.

**Role State Machine:**

```
Discovery → Active → Shortlisting → Interviewing → Offer → Placed
   ↓                                                          ↓
Cancelled                                              Fee Invoiced
                                                            ↓
                                                       Fee Collected
```

**Candidate-in-Role State Machine:**

```
Sourced → Screened → Evaluated → Shortlisted → Interviewing → Offered → Placed
   ↓         ↓           ↓            ↓              ↓           ↓
Rejected  Rejected   Rejected     Rejected       Rejected    Declined
```

**Business Rules:**

| Rule | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Max candidates shortlisted per role | 5 | Forces quality over volume |
| Client dossier response SLA | 5 business days | Keeps pipeline moving |
| Stale role flag threshold | 45 days with no placement | Triggers re-discovery |
| Fee invoice trigger | Candidate join date | Not offer date — protects against no-shows |
| Replacement guarantee | 90 days | If candidate leaves, one free replacement search |
| Referral reward trigger | Candidate completes 30 days | Prevents gaming |

**Revenue Calculation:**

```
estimated_fee = role.compensationMax * role.feePercentage
actual_fee    = candidate.actualCompensation * role.feePercentage

// Track: estimated vs actual fee delta — helps calibrate future pricing
```

### Engine 6: Referral Network Engine

**Anti-Abuse Rules:**

| Rule | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Max 3 referrals per person per role | Prevents spray-and-pray |
| Referrer cannot refer themselves | Obvious |
| Referrer must have > 80% profile completion | Ensures they're invested |
| First referrer gets credit (per candidate per role) | Prevents double-counting |
| 60-day cooling period after rejection | Can't re-refer a rejected candidate to any role for 60 days |

**Scout Score Formula:**

```
scout_score = (
  submitted * 1 +
  shortlisted * 3 +
  interviewed * 5 +
  hired * 10
) * success_rate_multiplier

where success_rate_multiplier = max(hired / submitted, 0.1)
```

**Example:**

| Referrer | Submitted | Shortlisted | Interviewed | Hired | Raw Score | Multiplier | Scout Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 10 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 39 | 0.10 | 3.9 |
| B | 3 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 29 | 0.33 | **9.6** |

Referrer B scores higher. Quality is rewarded over volume.

**Talent Scout Promotion:**

```
if scout_score > 8.0 AND total_referrals >= 5 AND hired >= 2:
    promote to Talent Scout
    Benefits: early access to roles, higher reward %, badge
```

## Implementation Plan — 4 Phases

### Phase 0: Validate Without Code (Week 1-2)

Do 2-3 searches manually using Google Sheets + Notion.

**Validate:**
- Can you decompose a role into Hiring DNA in < 30 minutes?
- Can you source 15+ candidates for a role in < 1 week?
- Do clients actually read and value the dossier format vs a resume?
- Will people refer candidates if you ask them?

**Output:** 1-2 placements (or near-placements) done entirely manually.

---

### Phase 1: Operational MVP (Week 3-6)

Build only what you **cannot do in a spreadsheet**.

**Build:**
- Candidate database with all 6 intelligence layers
- Role intake form with Hiring DNA structure
- Scoring + composite calculation
- Candidate dossier generator (the printable page)
- Basic pipeline kanban
- Auth (admin only)

**Don't build yet:** Trust Graph visualization, referral portal, client portal, analytics.

---

### Phase 2: Network Effects (Month 2-3)

You have 50+ candidates and 5+ active roles. Build the network.

**Build:**
- Trust Graph storage + basic visualization
- Referrer portal (sign up, see roles, submit referrals, track status)
- Scout Score calculation + leaderboard
- Client portal (view dossiers, track pipeline)
- Email notifications
- WhatsApp integration

---

### Phase 3: Intelligence Layer (Month 4-6)

You have 200+ candidates, 20+ completed roles, and placement outcome data.

**Build:**
- Trust propagation algorithm
- Matching suggestions (auto-suggest top 10 from graph for new roles)
- Career velocity scoring
- "Similar candidates" recommendations
- Analytics dashboard
- LinkedIn auto-populate

---

### Phase 4: Platform (Month 6-12)

**Build:**
- Talent mapping reports (sell to clients as a product)
- Compensation intelligence (aggregate anonymized data)
- Hiring outcome tracking (feed back into scores)
- API for ATS integrations
- Multi-recruiter support
